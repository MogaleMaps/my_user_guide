●

I smile a lot. It's just how I spread positivity — it doesn't mean I'm not taking something seriously.

●

I take constructive feedback well — just not in a group setting. DM me or set up a quick call instead.

If I need to present or actively participate in a meeting, please prep me beforehand — I show up much stronger with a heads-up.

An acknowledgement to my message goes a long way — even just a 👍, 👀 or ✅. It doesn't need to be an answer, just let me know you've seen it.

I ask a lot of questions — it helps me understand better. Please be patient with me.
